Mailbox routing

Channel routes choose the default service team for each mailbox, WhatsApp number, Meta account, or registered website and portal widget surface. AI intake routes can override that default when a classified intent meets the configured confidence threshold. Each mailbox route can also select the SMTP profile used when its team replies.
